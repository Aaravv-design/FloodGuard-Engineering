ABOUT FLOODGUARD

Innovation That Protects Lives

Our Mission

FloodGuard combines intelligent sensors, hydraulic engineering and sustainable materials to minimise flood damage and improve community resilience.

Our system reacts before floodwater reaches dangerous levels, creating a protective barrier around homes automatically.

🛡 Smart Protection

Automatic hydraulic deployment with intelligent flood detection.

🌍 Sustainable

Reusable aluminium barriers designed for long-term use.

⚡ Fast Response

Deploys in under 10 seconds after activation.

📡 Connected

Live monitoring and instant emergency alerts.
